The resurrection

Hieroglyphic writing died out in the 4th century CE. For 1,400 years no one alive could read it, until the Rosetta Stone cracked it open in 1822. This typewriter uses the uniliteral alphabet of the Manuel de Codage, the same sign list Egyptologists use today, rendered with the Unicode Egyptian Hieroglyphs block.
